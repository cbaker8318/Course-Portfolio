My Top 5 Favorite Beach Destinations

By Chris Baker

  1. Freeport, Bahamas

    Freeport has beautiful beaches, clear water, and great resorts.

  2. Caye Caulker, Belize

    Caye Caulker is known for its protected coral reef, snorkeling, and scuba diving.

  3. Destin, Florida

    Destin has white sand beaches and beautiful emerald-green water perfect for a family vacation.

  4. Hollywood Beach, Florida

    Hollywood Beach offers beautiful beaches, warm weather, with a lively boardwalk and plenty of walkable shops and restaurants.

  5. Rhodos, Greece

    Rhodes has beautiful beaches, historical sites, great food, and a rich and vibrant culture with some of the most welcoming people I have ever met.
